### Changed
- `lium signup` says the e-mail may already have an account when it cannot sign in after signing up, and points
  to `lium init` or a password reset. A newer server answers a signup on a taken e-mail like a new one.
- `lium transfer` and `Lium.transfer_credits` document `recipient_unavailable` (409) for an unknown, deleted or
  blocked recipient; the server no longer answers a transfer with `not_found`.
