### Fixed
- `Lium.exec(env=...)` / `lium exec -e KEY=VALUE`: a variable name `export` would refuse on the pod (a leading digit, `-`, a space, a trailing newline) is rejected locally — `ValueError` in the SDK, exit 2 with the accepted form in the CLI — instead of failing on the pod with `export: not a valid identifier`.
- `Lium.exec_all` reports a pod whose SSH connection failed as `{"pod": <id>, "error": ..., "success": False}`; the failure entry used to carry the `PodInfo` object under `"pod"` while successes carried the id.
