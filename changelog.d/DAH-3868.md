### Added
- `lium fund --alpha --netuid <N>` pays with alpha from any subnet Lium accepts, not only the primary one. The CLI checks the subnet against the accepted list before it asks for the coldkey password; a subnet that is not on the list exits 2 with `netuid_not_accepted` and the accepted netuids. Without `--netuid` the payment stays on Lium's primary subnet, as before. `--netuid` without `--alpha` exits 2 with `netuid_needs_alpha`.
- SDK: `Lium.alpha_subnets()` returns the accepted subnets (`AlphaSubnets`, with each subnet's name and symbol and the primary netuid), and `Lium.convert_alpha(usd, netuid=None)` quotes on the given subnet.
