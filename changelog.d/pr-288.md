### Fixed
- `lium provider node notice-period set` takes `--start` (ISO 8601 with a UTC offset), `--minutes N` for a maintenance window of up to 60 minutes or `--permanent` for a removal, and `--reason`, and sends them to the portal. `ProviderClient.create_notice_period` accepts `starting_at`, `period_in_minute`, `reason` and `permanent_removal`. The old `payload` dict argument (and a call with no fields) still works and emits a `DeprecationWarning`; a refused payload's hint names the offending field.
