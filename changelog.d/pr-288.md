### Fixed
- `lium provider node notice-period set` takes `--start` (ISO 8601 with a UTC offset), `--minutes N` for a maintenance window of up to 60 minutes or `--permanent` for a removal, and `--reason`, and sends them to the portal. `ProviderClient.create_notice_period` accepts `starting_at`, `period_in_minute`, `reason` and `permanent_removal`.
