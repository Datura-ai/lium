### Added
- `lium ps --sort created|name|status|price|spent|gpu|uptime [--reverse]`, `--filter KEY=VALUE` (repeatable; `status`, `name`, `huid`, `gpu`, `template`, `id`; case-insensitive prefix match), `--watch N` to refresh and `--wide` to force every column.
- `lium spend [--format json]`: per active pod the hourly price, uptime, estimated spend (price × wall time since creation; $0 for a PENDING pod, which is not billing yet) and start time, plus total burn per hour (RUNNING/REBOOT_PENDING/REBOOT_FAILED pods only, the statuses the platform bills; volume storage excluded), the balance and the runway at the current burn. The API does not report billed amounts, so the figures are labelled as estimates.

### Changed
- A sorted or filtered `lium ps` numbers the rows it shows, and `lium rm 1` (ssh, exec, scp too) after it means row 1 of that listing: the row-number snapshot (DAH-2559) follows the listing shown, in the order shown; `lium ps <pod>` shows no row number and leaves it alone.
- `lium ps` hides the Ports column on interactive terminals narrower than 120 columns (it made every row wrap) and says how to get it back (`--wide` or `--format json`); output to a pipe keeps every column.
