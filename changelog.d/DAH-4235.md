### Fixed
- `lium fund` works with bittensor 11, which a fresh install now gets. bittensor 11 removed `bt.utils`, `Balance.set_unit` and the `Subtensor` methods `transfer`, `transfer_stake`, `get_stake_info_for_coldkey` and `get_stake_movement_fee`. On bittensor 11, `lium fund` now:
  - checks addresses with `bt.wallets.is_bittensor_address`;
  - sends the TAO transfer and the alpha `transfer_stake` as bittensor 11 intents through `Subtensor.execute`;
  - reads free alpha as the hotkey's stake, less any miner collateral bonded on that position, capped by what the coldkey has available on the subnet (bittensor 11 locks per coldkey). The reads use one finalized block, so they agree with each other while the wallet moves stake.

  The stake-movement fee is not shown there, since bittensor 11 has no call for it. bittensor 9 and 10 use the same calls as before.
- `lium fund --alpha`: when the transfer call fails after it was submitted (for example the confirmation times out), the error is `transfer_failed` with the hint "Do not re-send yet: the transfer may have reached the chain", on every bittensor version. It used to be a generic error whose hint said "Retry", which could send the alpha twice.
