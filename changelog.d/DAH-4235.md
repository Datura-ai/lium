### Fixed
- `lium fund` works with bittensor 11, which a fresh install now gets. bittensor 11 removed `bt.utils`, `Balance.set_unit` and the `Subtensor` methods `transfer`, `transfer_stake`, `get_stake_info_for_coldkey` and `get_stake_movement_fee`. On bittensor 11, `lium fund` now:
  - checks addresses with `bt.wallets.is_bittensor_address`;
  - sends the TAO transfer and the alpha `transfer_stake` as bittensor 11 intents through `Subtensor.execute`;
  - reads free alpha as the hotkey's stake capped by what the coldkey has available on the subnet (bittensor 11 locks per coldkey).

  The stake-movement fee is not shown there, since bittensor 11 has no call for it. bittensor 9 and 10 use the same calls as before.
