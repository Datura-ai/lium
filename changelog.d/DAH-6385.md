### Added
- `lium fund --gamma -a <USD>` (DAH-6385): pays with SN51 gamma. It links the wallet's coldkey to the
  Lium account by signing a challenge, then mints gamma with the wallet's TAO naming Lium's spender;
  Lium credits 1 gamma = 1 USD within a few minutes. The mint fails, costing only the network fee, if
  the price moves more than 2% before it lands.
