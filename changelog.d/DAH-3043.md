### Changed
- `lium ls` drops columns by priority when the terminal is narrow instead of squeezing all of them: the index, Id, Config, `$/GPU·h` and Location are always shown and never squeezed or cut (a long country name may wrap); Tier, Download, VRAM, Max CUDA, Upload, RAM, CPUs, Disk and Ports follow as width allows, and a footer says how many are hidden (`--format json` has them all).
