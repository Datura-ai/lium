### Fixed
- `Lium.exec` (and so `lium exec`) no longer hangs, or raises `TimeoutError` with a timeout, when a command prints more than about 2 MiB: it now reads stdout and stderr while it waits for the exit status.
- `Lium.exec` keeps at most `max_output_bytes` (default 256 MiB) of stdout and stderr together; past it the channel is closed and `OutputLimitExceeded` is raised, instead of a runaway command filling this process's memory.
