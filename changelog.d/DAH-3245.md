### Added
- CLI: `lium audit --account [--action PREFIX] [--source portal|cli|sdk|mcp|admin|api]` shows the account audit log (`GET /account/audit`, lium-platform DAH-3245): one entry per request that changed something — pods, keys, logins, balance, settings, team members — with the client and the IP it came from (your own IPs only; 90 days). `--limit` is 1–500 here; `--cursor <next_cursor>` shows the next (older) page; `--json` prints the page with `next_cursor`. A session actor is shown with the first 8 characters of its user id, so team members are told apart. A backend without the route is named in the error.
- SDK: `Lium.audit_log(since=, until=, action=, source=, api_key_id=, resource_id=, cursor=, limit=)` returns `{"items", "next_cursor"}` from `GET /account/audit`.
