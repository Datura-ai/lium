### Added
- SDK: `PodInfo.estimated_ready_seconds`, `PodInfo.eta_basis` and `PodInfo.phase` — the backend's estimate of how long a PENDING pod still needs, what it rests on (`executor_history` | `class_median` | `cold_pull_estimate` | `fleet_default`) and the creation step it has reached; `PodInfo.eta_hint()` renders them as one line. `None` on older backends and once the pod is RUNNING.
- CLI: `lium up`'s progress line carries that estimate — `waiting for <pod>… PENDING (12 s) · est. ready in ~10 s (phase: pulling image)` — and is printed again whenever the phase moves; a `--timeout` that runs out while the pod is still starting says what the backend still expects.
