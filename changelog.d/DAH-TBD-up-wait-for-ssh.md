### Fixed
- `lium up` and `lium ssh` wait up to 60 s for the pod's SSH port to send an SSH banner before they open the session ("Waiting for SSH on <host>:<port>..."). A pod reports RUNNING before an sshd started by the image is listening, and the single ssh attempt used to fail. If the port still doesn't answer after 60 s, ssh is tried once anyway. When that attempt fails too, both commands exit 4 with `ssh_connection_failed`: the message says the pod is RUNNING and billing and names `lium ssh <huid>` and `lium rm <huid>`, and the `--json` / `LIUM_OUTPUT=json` envelope carries `pod_id`, `pod_name`, `ssh_port_answered: false` and `ssh_wait` (host, port, attempts, wait_seconds, last_problem). The pod is never removed. `lium up --json` and `--no-ssh` don't wait, because they open no session.
