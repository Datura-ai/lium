### Added
- `@lium.machine(keep_warm=<seconds>)`: the pod stays after a call and the next call — from this process or the next run of the script, which finds it by name — reuses it instead of renting and booting again (60–90 s and a boot's worth of billing per call saved); the server-side removal is re-armed to `keep_warm + 2 min` after every call, so a caller that never comes back leaks nothing. `f.remote(x)` (same as `f(x)`), `f.local(x)` (run here), `f.map(items)` (every item on one pod, rented once) and `f.close()` (remove the warm pod). `local=True` or `LIUM_MACHINE_LOCAL=1` runs the function in-process for tests and offline work. At interpreter exit, pods held for `.map()` are removed; `keep_warm` pods are left to their TTL.
