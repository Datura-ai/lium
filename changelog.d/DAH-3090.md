### Changed
- `lium ls` Disk column is now the host's **free** disk (`Disk free (Gb)`), which is what a pod's allocation is computed from; `--format json`: **`disk_gb` now means free space** (it was the total) and `disk_total_gb` carries the old value — a contract change for readers of `ls --format json`. The total was shown before and no pod ever gets it (524 GB listed → 130 GB `/workspace` measured).
