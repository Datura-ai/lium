### Changed
- `lium fund --gamma` works on subtensor runtime 493, which removes gamma deposit spenders: it then
  mints and spends the gamma at Lium's service in one batch signed by the coldkey (also spending
  gamma already in the deposit), then spends what the mint gave above its 2% price margin, so the
  whole payment is credited. A failed spend in the batch reverts the mint. On earlier runtimes it
  mints naming Lium's spender as before.
- `lium fund --gamma` signs every payment with the wallet's finalized nonce and a mortal era, and
  refuses to start while an earlier transaction from the wallet is in the pool or one it sent with an
  unknown outcome (a reply lost or interrupted, including bittensor 11's `submission_outcome_unknown`)
  has not settled in finalized state; such nonces are kept in `~/.lium/gamma_unresolved.json` until
  they settle in finalized state or finalized blocks pass their era (never by elapsed time), and are
  updated under a file lock. A failure or Ctrl-C before anything is broadcast records nothing. A remainder spend with an unknown outcome is reported as
  pending (`gamma_rest_pending` in JSON), not as unspent.
- `link_gamma_hotkey`: documents `service`, which servers that accept gamma spent at a service also return.
