### Changed
- `lium fund --gamma` works on subtensor runtime 493, which removes gamma deposit spenders: it then
  mints and spends the gamma at Lium's service in one batch signed by the coldkey, also spending
  gamma already in the deposit, and Lium credits what was spent. A failed spend reverts the mint.
  On earlier runtimes it mints naming Lium's spender as before.
- `link_gamma_hotkey` also returns `service`, the hotkey a deposit's gamma is spent at from runtime 493.
