### Changed
- `Lium.exec`, `stream_exec`, `upload` and `download` keep one SSH connection per pod open between calls, so a
  second command to the same pod starts after one channel open (1.07 s → 0.45 s per warm `exec` at 150 ms RTT).
  `Lium.close()` or `with Lium() as lium:` closes them; `LIUM_SSH_REUSE=0` connects per call.
- `lium exec` leaves an OpenSSH control master open for 10 minutes after the last command, and a later
  `lium exec` to that pod runs over it without listing pods (1.75 s → 0.72 s per warm command at 150 ms RTT).
  `LIUM_SSH_PERSIST=<seconds>` sets the idle time; `LIUM_SSH_PERSIST=0` connects per command. `lium rm`,
  `lium reboot` and `Lium.switch_template` close the pod's master. `lium exec --json` reports `exit_code` 255
  for a command killed by a signal, as `ssh` does.
- A warm `@lium.machine` call reuses the connection it already has, skips `GET /pods`, and sends one TTL
  request fewer (5.71 s → 3.61 s per warm call at 150 ms RTT).

### Added
- `scripts/bench_warm_exec.py` times warm commands against a local sshd behind a delay proxy and a mock API.
