### Fixed
- `lium up <node>` and `Lium.get_executor()` resolve the HUID `lium ls` prints (`cosmic-hawk-f2`) as well as the UUID, against the same listing `ls` returns. A node that resolves to nothing is reported as "not in the current listing (looked up by UUID and HUID)" with the id that was looked up and a pointer to `lium ls --format json`, instead of a bare "not found". A HUID that several listed nodes share (HUIDs are short client-side hashes) is refused with the UUIDs to choose from, rather than renting the first match.
