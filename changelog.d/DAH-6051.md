### Fixed
- `lium mine --register` with a token from a staging or dev portal starts that stack's executor (`docker-compose.dev.yml`) and trusts its central miner (`DEFAULT_MINER_HOTKEY`), so the node can be verified there. The choice is kept in `.env` (`LIUM_DEV_STACK`), so a later `lium mine` without `--register` keeps it. Prod tokens change nothing.
