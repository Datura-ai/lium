### Changed
- `lium provider billing list` shows your own daily earnings ledger (`GET /provider-ledger/daily`, one row per UTC day
  and kind) and takes `--from` / `--to`. `--all`, `--miner-hotkey`, `--page` and `--limit` are removed, because the
  public `/billing` reads they used are being retired.
- `ProviderClient.billing_history()` takes `start_day` / `end_day` and reads the same ledger.
