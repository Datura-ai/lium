### Added
- `lium up --no-backup` rents a pod without Lium's automatic volume backup, and `lium up` says before renting
  that backups are on and how to turn them off. The SDK's `up`, `rent`, `rental` and `up_cluster` take
  `enable_backups` (default `True`).
- `lium clusters up --no-backup` does the same for every member, and the MCP `rent_pod` tool takes
  `enable_backups` (default `true`).
