### Added
- `lium gpu-splitting setup` and the storage step of `lium mine` work on single-disk hosts (DAH-5046):
  with no spare disk or partition, Docker storage goes into a preallocated XFS image file on the
  local filesystem with the most free space (free space minus a reserve of 10%, at least 20 GiB;
  refused when it would keep under 140 GiB free once the current Docker data moves into it — the
  marketplace's 100 GiB free after ~40 GiB of node images — or when its filesystem would fall under 1.5x
  the host's GPU VRAM, the validator's idle-incentive floor, with XFS metadata and the validator's
  rounding counted in; the mounted filesystem's total and, after the restore, its free space are checked
  again before the backup is removed), mounted at `/var/lib/docker` through a loop device with `pquota`
  and `nofail` (a missing file must not boot the host into emergency mode). A docker.service drop-in makes
  Docker wait for the mount; a udev rule turns on direct I/O on the loop device and refuses
  discard, so `fstrim` cannot punch holes back into the preallocated file. The file is created
  exclusively with mode 0600 in a root-only directory. `--file` and `--size` choose the path and
  size; `check` prints the plan. Docker 29 hosts on the containerd image store are switched to
  `overlay2`, the only driver that enforces `--storage-opt size`.
