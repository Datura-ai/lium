### Added
- `lium gpu-splitting setup` and the storage step of `lium mine` work on single-disk hosts (DAH-5046):
  with no spare disk or partition, Docker storage goes into a preallocated XFS image file on the
  local filesystem with the most free space (free space minus a reserve of 10%, at least 20 GiB),
  mounted at `/var/lib/docker` through a loop device with `pquota`. A docker.service drop-in makes
  Docker wait for the mount and turns on direct I/O on the loop device. `--file` and `--size`
  choose the path and size; `check` prints the plan. Docker 29 hosts on the containerd image
  store are switched to `overlay2`, the only driver that enforces `--storage-opt size`.
