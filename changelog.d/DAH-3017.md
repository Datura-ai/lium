### Changed
- `@lium.machine`: the per-call venv is replaced by one venv per requirements list (`/tmp/lium-venv-<hash>`), created with `--system-site-packages` so the image's own torch/CUDA stack is visible (`import torch` without `requirements` used to be `ModuleNotFoundError`, and `requirements=["torch", ...]` re-downloaded ~3 GB of torch on every call), populated once and reused by later calls on the same pod; venv creation and `pip install` are one SSH round trip instead of two.
