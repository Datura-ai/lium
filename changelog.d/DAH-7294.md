### Added
- `lium provider node intro-price <NODE_ID> --price P --hours H` sets an intro price on a Secure node: a new rental pays P per GPU-hour for its first H hours (24 to 720), then the node's price, and both are fixed when it is rented. `--clear` removes the intro price, and rentals already running keep their terms. SDK: `ProviderClient.set_node_intro_price(node_id, intro_price_per_gpu, intro_hours)` (`PUT /executors/{id}/intro-price`; `None, None` clears it). Needs the provider portal release that adds the endpoint.
