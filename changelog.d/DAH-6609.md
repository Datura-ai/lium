### Fixed
- `Lium.quote` docstring and agent docs: the balance must be more than 15 minutes of the new pod and of each open pod; some older accounts need more. Who rents decides the exemption from this pod-cost reserve: admins, and a validator renting a node on its own validator, are exempt. Who pays must have a balance above zero, except the Lium validator paying from its own account.
- `LiumInsufficientBalanceError` docstring: `required` and `available` report the amounts printed in the rent refusal.
- `lium quote` no longer says "No balance floor applies" for an exempt renter: it says the rent is exempt from the pod-cost reserve and that the paying account still needs a balance above zero, except the Lium validator paying from its own account. A free pod, when the hourly prices of the open pods counted toward the reserve sum to zero, shows "Quoted pod-cost reserve: $0. The rent API checks the paying account separately", since the server sends the same zeros for an exempt and a non-exempt renter.
- `LiumInsufficientBalanceError.available` keeps the sign of a negative balance (the refusal prints `-$0.30`, personal or workspace wording); it was `None` before.
- `LiumInsufficientBalanceError.required` reads both refusal wordings, "requires at least $X" and "requires more than $X", and holds the amount printed.

### Changed
- `lium signup` no longer mentions a signup credit. It tells you to check the balance and fund the account before renting.
