### Fixed
- `Lium.quote` docstring and agent docs: the balance must be more than 15 minutes of the new pod and of each open pod (running, starting, rebooting, stopped, failed to reboot, or in Failed status, a failed creation not included; pods on nodes of the payer's own validator do not count), or more than 60 minutes while the payer has an open signup credit and either has no completed card, crypto, TAO or alpha top-ups or has less than the minimum top-up in those top-ups, bonuses included. Admins, and validators renting a node on their own validator, are exempt from this pod-cost reserve but still need a payer balance above $0; only the Lium validator renting its own nodes needs no balance. In production, new accounts get no signup credit.
- `LiumInsufficientBalanceError.required` is read from the rent refusal's "requires more than $X" wording as well as the older "requires at least $X"; the balance must be more than `required` for both wordings.
