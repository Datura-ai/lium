### Fixed
- `Lium.quote` docstring and agent docs: the balance must be more than 15 minutes of the new pod and of each open pod (running, starting, rebooting, stopped, failed to reboot, or in Failed status, a failed creation not included; pods on nodes of the payer's own validator do not count), or more than 60 minutes while the payer has an open signup credit and either has no completed card, crypto, TAO or alpha top-ups or has less than the minimum top-up in those top-ups, bonuses included. Admins, and validators renting a node on their own validator, are exempt from this pod-cost reserve but still need a payer balance above $0; only the Lium validator renting its own nodes and paying from its own account needs no balance; through a workspace, its billing owner still needs a balance above $0. In production, new accounts get no signup credit.
- `LiumInsufficientBalanceError` docstring: on current servers `required` is the smallest whole-cent balance that rents; older servers printed the 15-minute cost rounded to the cent and refused a balance at or below the unrounded cost.
