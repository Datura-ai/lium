### Changed
- `gpu_type` for the RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell (Workstation or Server Edition) is now `RTXPRO6000` instead of `Edition` in `lium ls`, `lium ps`, their `--format json` output and the SDK's `ExecutorInfo`; `lium ls --gpu` and `lium up --gpu` accept `RTXPRO6000`, `pro6000` and `rtx pro 6000` for it. The 84 GB `RTX PRO 6000D` keeps its suffix (`RTXPRO6000D`, alias `pro6000d`), so `--gpu pro6000` does not rent it.
