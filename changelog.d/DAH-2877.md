### Added
- `lium up` compares the GPU count the pod is billed for (the pod's own count from `/pods`, so a GPU-split rental is judged by what it got, not by the whole host) with the requested count once the pod is ready and exits non-zero on a mismatch, naming both numbers and the node. The requested count is `--count`, or without it the chosen node's free GPU count: a rent that names no count takes every free GPU, which on a partially rented split host is less than the host total.
- `lium up --verify-gpus` additionally runs `nvidia-smi -L` inside the pod over SSH — retrying the connection for up to 90 s while sshd comes up — and fails when the GPUs it lists differ from the billed count. A failed command (`nvidia-smi` missing, driver not loaded) or no connection is reported as `gpu_verification_failed`, never as a mismatch.
- `lium up --strict-gpus` removes the pod automatically when a GPU-count mismatch is detected; a pod that could not be checked is kept.
