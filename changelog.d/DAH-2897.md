### Fixed
- `Lium.logs()` goes through `_request` like every other call: a 403/4xx message uses the parsed server detail instead of the raw body, a 5xx or dropped connection while opening the stream is retried, and the status-to-exception mapping lives in one place (`Lium._raise_for_status`). `_request(..., timeout=)` can override the 30 s default.
